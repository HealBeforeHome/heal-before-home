Interventional Radiology in the Philippines

Precision treatment through advanced imaging.

Interventional radiology uses advanced imaging to guide minimally invasive procedures used to diagnose and treat a range of conditions.

Depending on the procedure, this approach may involve smaller incisions, shorter hospital stays, or different recovery requirements compared with conventional surgery.

Heal Before Home coordinates the non-clinical arrangements surrounding interventional radiology services in the Philippines for international and discerning local guests.

From consultations and diagnostic scheduling to accommodation, private transportation, recovery support, and follow-up logistics, we organize the surrounding details around the plan established by your healthcare team.

Interventional Radiology Options

01

Vascular Interventions

Minimally invasive, image-guided procedures used to diagnose and treat narrowed, blocked, or abnormal blood vessels.

02

Embolization

A targeted procedure that reduces or blocks blood flow to selected areas to help treat bleeding, vascular conditions, or certain tumors.

03

TACE (Transarterial Chemoembolization)

A treatment that delivers chemotherapy directly to a tumor while reducing its blood supply, commonly used for selected liver cancers.

04

SIRT / Y-90 Radioembolization

A highly targeted treatment that delivers radioactive microspheres directly to certain liver tumors through the arterial system.

05

Thrombectomy & Thrombolysis

Catheter-based techniques used to remove or dissolve blood clots in appropriately selected patients.

06

Image-Guided Biopsy

Uses advanced imaging to guide precise tissue sampling for diagnostic evaluation.

07

Image-Guided Drainage

Minimally invasive imaging techniques used for drainage, catheter placement, and related therapeutic procedures.

08

Interventional Oncology

Targeted cancer treatments that may include embolization, ablation, and other image-guided therapies.

Before choosing an interventional radiology provider

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Interventional radiology procedures can vary considerably in technique, clinical indication, preparation, recovery, and follow-up. A thorough consultation should help you understand why a procedure is being recommended, the alternatives available, potential risks and benefits, and what to expect before and after treatment.

Clinical Expertise & Treatment Planning

  • Specialist Experience: Who will perform my procedure, and what are their qualifications, board certifications, and experience with this specific treatment?
  • Treatment Rationale: Why is this procedure being recommended for my condition, and what alternative treatment options should I understand?
  • Diagnostic Requirements: Which imaging, laboratory tests, or other diagnostics are required before the procedure, and why?

Procedure, Risks & Recovery

  • Sedation & Anesthesia: Will the procedure require local anesthesia, sedation, general anesthesia, or another approach?
  • Risks & Expected Outcomes: What are the potential risks, contraindications, complications, limitations, and expected outcomes based on my individual health and condition?
  • Observation & Recovery: How long should I expect to remain under observation, in hospital, or near the treating facility following the procedure?

Travel & Follow-Up

  • Travel Readiness: When may I safely fly or undertake long-distance travel following the procedure, based on my recovery?
  • When to Seek Medical Attention: Which symptoms or complications should prompt me to contact my treating team or seek urgent medical care?
  • Records & Documentation: Will I receive copies of procedural reports, relevant imaging, pathology results where applicable, discharge instructions, and physician recommendations?
Planning your journey

Interventional Radiology Planning & Recovery Phases

An interventional radiology procedure may involve specialist consultations, laboratory testing, diagnostic imaging, preparation instructions, sedation or anesthesia, post-procedure observation, and follow-up. Heal Before Home coordinates the surrounding non-clinical arrangements around the clinical plan established by your healthcare providers.

01

Phase 1

Before Your Procedure

The surrounding logistics are coordinated in advance around your procedure schedule and preparation instructions.

  • Appointment & Travel Coordination: Organizing specialist consultations, diagnostic appointments, accommodation, and private transportation around your treatment schedule.
  • Pre-Procedure Scheduling: Structuring your itinerary around fasting requirements, medication instructions, laboratory testing, or other preparation requirements provided by your medical team.
02

Phase 2

During Your Stay

Day-to-day logistics are coordinated across the facilities involved in your treatment and recovery.

  • Procedure-Day Coordination: Organizing private transportation and scheduling around your procedure, consultations, imaging, and provider-directed observation period.
  • Multi-Location Logistics: Coordinating appointments and transportation across clinics, laboratories, imaging facilities, hospitals, or treatment centers involved in your care.
03

Phase 3

As Your Recovery Progresses

Your providers may recommend additional testing, consultation, observation, or changes to your schedule based on your recovery or procedure findings.

  • Additional Testing & Consultations: Coordinating further imaging, laboratory testing, specialist consultations, or other appointments recommended by your treating team.
  • Schedule Adjustments: Updating accommodation, private transportation, and itinerary arrangements when additional time or follow-up is required.
  • Recovery Support: Coordinating non-clinical recovery services and surrounding logistics in accordance with instructions provided by your healthcare team.
04

Phase 4

Returning Home & Follow-Up

Before departure, Heal Before Home helps organize the non-clinical arrangements surrounding the follow-up plan established by your treating team.

  • Results & Procedure Review: Coordinating consultations where your providers review procedure findings, relevant imaging or pathology results, recovery instructions, and next steps.
  • Records & Documentation: Helping organize access to procedure reports, imaging files, treatment summaries, discharge information, and other relevant records.
  • Follow-Up Coordination: Organizing remote consultations, future appointments, or repeat testing recommended after you return home.
  • Return-Travel Logistics: Coordinating transportation and travel arrangements around your provider’s guidance regarding travel readiness.
Recovery alignment

RN-Led Preparation & Recovery Support

For interventional radiology journeys where the treating provider’s plan includes substantial activity or mobility restrictions, staged recovery requirements, medication routines, or multiple follow-up requirements, RN-led Preparation & Recovery Coaching is a required component of the Heal Before Home program.

The program supports your execution of provider-issued recovery instructions, which may include activity limitations, mobility progression, rest, hydration, nutrition, medication routines, scheduled follow-up, and practical preparation for the early recovery period.

Heal Before Home can also coordinate accommodation, private transportation, companion or caregiver support, and surrounding daily logistics around those requirements.

For lower-complexity interventional procedures that do not require HBH’s enhanced recovery framework, the standard HBH coordination model applies.

Supporting the Recovery Plan

RN-led coaching is designed to support consistent follow-through with the recovery plan established by your treating healthcare team. Clinical monitoring, medical assessment, treatment decisions, medication changes, and changes to activity or recovery restrictions remain entirely with your healthcare provider.

Recovery & aftercare

Interventional Radiology Follow-Up & Recovery Support

Follow-up after an interventional radiology procedure varies according to the treatment performed and your individual recovery.

Depending on your provider’s recommendations, follow-up may include imaging, laboratory testing, wound or catheter-site review, medication guidance, specialist consultation, or additional monitoring before you return home.

Heal Before Home coordinates the non-clinical arrangements around those next steps, keeping appointments, transportation, accommodation, documentation, and follow-up logistics organized throughout your stay in the Philippines and as you prepare to return home.

Results & Procedure Review

  • Provider Follow-Up: Coordinating appointments where your treating team can review the procedure, relevant findings, imaging or pathology results, and next steps.
  • Additional Testing: Arranging appointments and transportation if your provider recommends further imaging, laboratory testing, or specialist consultation.
  • Recovery Scheduling: Adjusting surrounding logistics when additional observation, follow-up, or recovery time is recommended.

Preparing for Follow-Up at Home

  • Records & Documentation: Helping organize access to procedure reports, imaging, pathology results where applicable, discharge information, and provider recommendations.
  • Follow-Up Planning: Coordinating future appointments, remote consultations, or repeat testing recommended by your treating team.
  • Continuity at Home: Helping organize the information you may need to share with healthcare professionals after you return home.

Clear clinical boundaries

Your treating interventional radiologists and other licensed healthcare providers remain responsible for clinical decisions, procedural care, diagnostic interpretation, medications, treatment recommendations, and follow-up instructions. Heal Before Home does not practice medicine. We coordinate the travel, accommodation, transportation, scheduling, documentation, and recovery logistics surrounding your provider-led care.

Heal Before Home does not assess medical suitability, recommend or direct treatment, or replace your licensed healthcare providers. Where requested, we may help you explore vetted provider options and facilitate introductions for direct consultation and treatment planning.
