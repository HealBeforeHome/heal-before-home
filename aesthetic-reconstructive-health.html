Aesthetic, Regenerative & Reconstructive Care in the Philippines

Aesthetic care may include approaches ranging from skin health and regenerative therapies to minimally invasive treatments and selected surgical procedures.

The appropriate option depends on your individual health, anatomy, treatment goals, and the recommendation of a qualified healthcare provider.

Heal Before Home coordinates the non-clinical arrangements surrounding aesthetic, regenerative, and reconstructive care in the Philippines for international and discerning local guests.

From consultations and appointment scheduling to accommodation, private transportation, recovery support, and follow-up logistics, we organize the surrounding details so you can focus on your treatment and recovery.

Selected surgical requests may require an additional program-fit review before Heal Before Home accepts coordination.

Aesthetic, Regenerative & Reconstructive Options

01

Skin Health & Natural Rejuvenation

Provider-led skin programs may address texture, pigmentation, sun damage, acne scarring, signs of aging, and overall skin health. Options may range from skin health, rejuvenation, regenerative and minimally invasive treatments to selected reconstructive or surgical procedures.

02

Regenerative Aesthetic Treatments

Selected providers may offer regenerative and biostimulatory approaches designed to support skin quality, collagen production, tissue health, and natural rejuvenation. Options may include PRP or PRF, collagen-stimulating treatments, polynucleotides/PDRN, and other provider-led biologic or regenerative therapies where appropriate.

03

Laser & Energy-Based Treatments

Laser, radiofrequency, ultrasound, light-based, and other energy technologies may be used for selected skin, pigmentation, resurfacing, tightening, vascular, or rejuvenation concerns. The appropriate technology and treatment plan should be determined by a qualified provider.

04

Minimally Invasive Facial Treatments

Selected non-surgical options may include neuromodulator injections, dermal fillers, biostimulatory treatments, microneedling, skin resurfacing, and other minimally invasive procedures where clinically appropriate. The goal should be an individualized approach rather than treatment for treatment’s sake.

05

Scar Revision & Reconstructive Care

Reconstructive care may address scars or changes resulting from injury, burns, previous surgery, congenital conditions, cancer treatment, or other medical circumstances. Treatment may range from non-surgical approaches to surgical revision depending on the nature of the concern.

06

Selected Facial & Body Procedures

Selected aesthetic surgery and facial rejuvenation procedures may be considered for clients seeking subtle, individualized changes while preserving a natural appearance. Options may include eyelid, facial, neck, or other carefully selected procedures. Treatment suitability, technique, and recovery requirements are determined by the treating specialist.

07

Major Aesthetic & Reconstructive Surgery

More extensive aesthetic or reconstructive procedures may involve anesthesia, longer recovery, greater mobility limitations, or closer follow-up. Heal Before Home considers these requests individually based on the procedure, provider recommendations, anticipated recovery needs, and overall program fit.

For major elective surgical journeys accepted by Heal Before Home, Confidence & Transition Coaching is a required component of the HBH program.

08

Confidence & Transition Coaching

Changes in appearance can carry emotional as well as physical considerations.

For clients considering selected aesthetic procedures, Heal Before Home can coordinate private coaching designed to explore expectations, prepare for the experience of recovery, and support adjustment before and after treatment.

  • Intent & Expectations: Exploring what you hope to gain from the experience and separating realistic aesthetic change from broader life expectations.
  • Emotional Preparation: Preparing for temporary changes in appearance, downtime, reliance on others, and the emotional experience of recovery.
  • Transition Support: Private virtual coaching before and after treatment to support confidence and adjustment as recovery progresses.
  • Life Beyond the Procedure: Encouraging reflection on well-being, identity, confidence, and the wider life you are returning to after treatment.

Before choosing an aesthetic, regenerative or reconstructive provider

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Aesthetic, regenerative, and reconstructive treatments can vary considerably in technique, evidence, expected results, recovery, and clinical risk. A thorough consultation should help you understand why a particular option is being recommended, reasonable alternatives, potential risks and limitations, and what to expect during recovery.

Provider Expertise & Treatment Rationale

  • Provider Qualifications: Who will perform my treatment or procedure, and what are their qualifications, board certifications or relevant certifications, and experience with this specific treatment?
  • Treatment Rationale: Why is this treatment or procedure being recommended for my anatomy, condition, and goals?
  • Alternative Approaches: Are there less invasive, non-surgical, or other appropriate alternatives I should understand before deciding?
  • Evidence & Innovation: What evidence supports the recommended treatment, and is any part of it considered emerging, experimental, or primarily wellness-focused?

Procedure, Risks & Safety

  • Anesthesia & Sedation: Will anesthesia or sedation be required, and who will administer and monitor it?
  • Treatment Facility: Where will the procedure be performed, and what medical support is available should a complication occur?
  • Risks & Limitations: What are the potential risks, side effects, contraindications, complications, and limitations associated with the treatment?
  • Realistic Expectations: What results may be reasonable based on my individual anatomy, health, age, and circumstances?

Expectations & Readiness

  • Why Now: What is motivating me to consider this procedure at this point in my life?
  • What Do I Expect to Change: Are my expectations focused on the physical outcome, or am I hoping the procedure will change other areas of my life?
  • Recovery Readiness: Am I prepared for temporary swelling, bruising, reduced activity, uncertainty, or changes in appearance while healing?
  • Support: Do I have the practical and emotional support I may need during recovery?
  • Independent Guidance: Would speaking privately with an appropriately qualified body-image or mental-health professional help me explore my expectations before making a final decision?

For clients considering major elective aesthetic surgery, Heal Before Home offers a private Readiness & Expectations Consultation with an independent, appropriately qualified professional before the HBH surgical journey begins.

Recovery, Travel & Follow-Up

  • Recovery Timeline: What should I expect during recovery, and how long should I plan to remain near the treating provider?
  • Travel Readiness: When will my treating team determine that I am ready to fly or undertake long-distance travel?
  • Follow-Up Planning: What follow-up will be required before and after I return home?
  • Post-Treatment Concerns: Who should I contact if I have questions or develop a clinical concern after treatment?

Understanding Your Options Matters

A reputable provider should be able to explain the available treatment options, reasonable alternatives, expected benefits and limitations, potential risks, recovery requirements, and realistic outcomes while giving you sufficient time to make an informed decision.

Planning your journey

Planning & Recovery Phases Beyond the Procedure

Traveling for aesthetic, regenerative, or reconstructive care may involve consultations, pre-treatment testing, treatment or surgery, recovery, and structured follow-up. Heal Before Home coordinates the surrounding non-clinical arrangements around the plan established by your healthcare provider.

01

Phase 1

Before Treatment

The surrounding logistics are coordinated in advance around your treatment schedule and any preparation instructions provided by your healthcare team.

  • Provider & Appointment Coordination: Organizing initial consultations, pre-treatment appointments, accommodation, and private transportation.
  • Program-Fit Review: For selected elective surgical procedures, Heal Before Home may conduct an internal coordination review before accepting the case and developing the travel itinerary.
  • Pre-Treatment Planning: Structuring your itinerary around diagnostic testing, consultations, and other preparation requirements established by your provider.
02

Phase 2

During Your Stay

Day-to-day logistics are coordinated across the clinics, treatment facilities, and other locations involved in your program.

  • Multi-Provider Coordination: Organizing appointments, private transportation, accommodation, and companion arrangements across multiple providers or locations.
  • Schedule Management: Keeping the surrounding itinerary organized around treatments, consultations, follow-up appointments, and any provider-recommended downtime.
03

Phase 3

Recovery & Aftercare

Recovery varies considerably depending on the treatment or procedure. Your healthcare provider remains responsible for instructions regarding medications, wound care, activity, positioning, follow-up, and other clinical requirements.

  • Recovery Environment Coordination: Arranging recovery-focused accommodation, transportation, meal support, mobility assistance, and caregiver or nursing services where recommended or requested.
  • Follow-Up Coordination: Organizing transportation and scheduling for post-treatment checks and other appointments requested by your provider.
  • Extended-Stay Logistics: Adjusting accommodation, transportation, and itinerary arrangements if your provider recommends additional recovery time in the Philippines.
04

Phase 4

Returning Home & Follow-Up

Before departure, Heal Before Home helps organize the non-clinical arrangements surrounding your provider’s follow-up and travel recommendations.

  • Travel Readiness Coordination: Scheduling final appointments so your treating provider can determine when you are medically ready to travel.
  • Records & Documentation: Helping organize access to procedure reports, implant information where applicable, provider instructions, imaging, and other relevant records.
  • Follow-Up Planning: Coordinating remote consultations, future appointments, or additional monitoring recommended after you return home.
  • Return-Travel Logistics: Organizing transportation and travel arrangements around the timeline established by your treating team.

Clear clinical boundaries

Your treating healthcare providers remain responsible for clinical assessment, treatment recommendations, procedural care, medications, aftercare instructions, and all other medical decisions. Heal Before Home does not practice medicine. We coordinate the appointments, transportation, accommodation, hospitality, recovery support, documentation, follow-up logistics, and return-home arrangements surrounding your provider-led care.

Heal Before Home does not assess medical suitability, recommend or direct treatment, or replace your licensed healthcare providers. Where requested, we may help you explore vetted provider options and facilitate introductions for direct consultation and treatment planning.
