Answers before you begin

The most common questions about Heal Before Home and our personalized care coordination.

Heal Before Home provides personalized coordination for individuals seeking healthcare, longevity, wellness and recovery experiences in the Philippines. We bring together the services surrounding care — planning, accommodation and private transportation through to nutrition, recovery support, follow-up, and access to carefully considered independent providers when needed.

No. Heal Before Home does not determine medical suitability or provide medical treatment, clinical advice, diagnoses or medical recommendations. Medical decisions remain between you and your licensed healthcare providers. When needed, we can facilitate introductions to independent providers for you to explore directly.

Heal Before Home is designed for individuals seeking a more coordinated, comfortable and personalized healthcare or wellness experience in the Philippines. Whether you are travelling from abroad, seeking care locally, accompanying a loved one, or preparing for recovery, we coordinate the experience around your needs and your provider's guidance.

We take a broader view of the healthcare experience — bringing together provider access where needed, planning, hospitality, logistics, recovery support, follow-up and the transition home. Rather than focusing on one part of care, we coordinate the experience surrounding it.

Absolutely. If you already have a healthcare provider, Heal Before Home can work alongside you and your care team to coordinate the services and logistics surrounding your existing care plan.

We consider providers based on professional credentials where applicable, reputation, patient-safety practices, communication, service standards, transparency, and alignment with Heal Before Home's values. Our review does not replace your own due diligence or your independent relationship with your healthcare provider.

We support your research with transparent information and carefully considered provider options, and we encourage you to compare options, ask questions and review credentials before making a decision.

We believe trust is earned through transparency, clear communication and thoughtful coordination. We are equally clear about the boundaries of our role: Heal Before Home designs and coordinates the experience around your care, while medical and clinical services are provided independently by qualified healthcare professionals.

Start with an enquiry and an introductory conversation. We will learn where you are in your journey, whether you already have a provider, what you are considering, and the level of support you may need. From there, we can guide you through the appropriate next steps.

Heal Before Home may coordinate follow-up logistics, provider communication, records where appropriate, virtual follow-up planning and other agreed continuity support as you transition home. Medical follow-up and clinical decisions remain with your healthcare providers.

We welcome enquiries from healthcare, longevity, wellness, recovery, hospitality and transportation providers who share our standards for quality, service and client care. Visit our For Providers page to introduce your organization and apply for consideration.
