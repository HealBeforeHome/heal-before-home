The Experience

What we coordinate

Medical travel involves far more than the treatment itself. We bring the moving parts around your care together, preparation, arrival, appointments, recovery and your return home, with one goal of exceptional coordination and zero unnecessary friction.

Before departure

Your journey begins before takeoff

Your travel plans should work around your care, not the other way around. We bring together arrival timing, appointments, accommodation, recovery needs and return travel so your itinerary is in place before you leave.

On the ground

Private chauffeur transportation

Your journey continues beyond the airport. Private transportation is arranged around your itinerary, from arrival and accommodation to provider appointments, follow-up visits and your return to the airport.

Where you stay

A place designed for recovery

We arrange private, comfortable accommodation suited to your treatment, length of stay and preferred level of support, from well-appointed hotel suites to private residential or villa settings when appropriate.

During your stay

Support throughout your stay

Depending on your needs, services may include recovery-focused meals, nursing or personal assistance, pharmacy essentials, housekeeping, laundry and follow-up transportation, so you can focus on rest.

Behind the journey

Virtual Journey Assistance

Behind the scenes, HBH provides ongoing administrative support for appointment confirmations, itinerary updates, document coordination, service requests, and the practical details that may arise throughout your journey.

After you return

Continuity Once You Are Home

We coordinate follow-up logistics, provider communication and records where appropriate, so the transition home is as considered as the arrival.

All clinical follow-up, symptom monitoring, and medical guidance remain exclusively with your licensed healthcare providers.

Phase 04

Returning Home & Follow-Up

Before departure, Heal Before Home (HBH) coordinates the administrative transition from your health program in the Philippines to continued care at home, with particular attention to records access and follow-up.

  • Data Portability Management
  • Medical Records & Imaging Retrieval
  • Secure Records Hand-Off

Finding a healthcare provider is only one component of a medical journey. Traditional travel frameworks are designed primarily around flights, hotels, and leisure itineraries. Heal Before Home is built around the more complex timing, recovery, documentation, and support requirements that can surround provider-led care.

  • Clinical Timeline Coordination: Synchronizing your daily itinerary, private transportation, rest periods, and surrounding arrangements around provider-established fasting requirements, preparation guidelines, diagnostic timing, and multi-provider schedules.
  • Recovery-Appropriate Accommodation: Selecting accommodations and private recovery spaces based on accessibility, privacy, room layout, companion or caregiver needs, and provider-recommended environmental considerations such as light, noise, and rest requirements.
  • Nutrition Around Provider Guidance: Coordinating specialized meal delivery and dietary arrangements around instructions established by your healthcare provider or dietitian before testing, treatment, and recovery.
  • Data Portability & Continuity: With your authorization, Heal Before Home coordinates the retrieval and secure organization of available medical records, laboratory results, imaging files, and provider documentation generated during your journey, helping make relevant information easier to access and share with your healthcare professionals at home.

For complex journeys

Complex Journey Planning Retainer

Selected higher-complexity healthcare journeys begin with an additional planning stage before detailed coordination begins. This allows Heal Before Home to assess the logistical, recovery, travel, scheduling, and professional support requirements needed to structure the journey appropriately.

Selected higher-complexity healthcare journeys require additional planning before detailed coordination begins. Once Heal Before Home confirms that your journey falls within one of these tracks, a $300 USD Complex Journey Planning Retainer is required to activate this stage of your program.

The retainer supports the initial architecture of your journey, including review of provider requirements, appointment and treatment sequencing, international travel considerations, accommodation and recovery planning, support requirements, and identification of any specialized preparation or professional support needed before, during, or after treatment.

The Complex Journey Planning Retainer is required for:

  • Major elective aesthetic surgery: including major facial or body procedures and other extensive elective surgical journeys accepted into the HBH major-surgery program.
  • Complex dental surgery and rehabilitation: including full-arch implant surgery, extensive bone grafting, major oral rehabilitation, and multi-stage surgical dental treatment involving substantial recovery requirements.
  • Advanced longevity and regenerative programs: where the provider-established program involves more extensive preparation, recovery requirements, multiple treatment stages, or significant international travel.
  • Comprehensive executive health and diagnostic programs: when the program involves multi-day testing, provider-directed fasting, tightly sequenced diagnostics, or significant international travel requiring additional pre-assessment planning.
  • Selected interventional radiology procedures: when the provider’s plan includes substantial activity or mobility restrictions, staged recovery requirements, medication routines, or multiple follow-up appointments requiring enhanced recovery coordination.
  • Complex fertility and reproductive-care journeys: when treatment involves multiple stages, closely timed appointments, laboratory coordination, monitoring, extended stays, or other significant logistical requirements.
  • Other complex international healthcare journeys: where multiple providers, extended stays, specialized recovery arrangements, or significant coordination requirements make detailed planning necessary before the program can be finalized.
Applied to your HBH program

If your journey is accepted for coordination and you proceed with an eligible Heal Before Home program, the full $300 USD retainer is credited toward your HBH program fee.

The retainer is collected only after your initial consultation and after Heal Before Home confirms that your journey requires Complex Journey Planning.

If you choose to pause or discontinue your journey after detailed planning has begun, the retainer is retained to cover the professional and administrative resources already committed to your file.
