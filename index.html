The Heal Before Home Experience

Your journey at a glance

Whether you are still exploring options or have already found a provider, we meet you where you are and bring the next steps together around your care, stay, recovery and return home.

01

Your starting point

Discover

We begin by understanding where you are, what you are considering and what matters most to you. Once you complete the enquiry form, we arrange a personalized consultation to discuss your next steps.

02

Planning ahead

Plan

We organize the details around your provider's care plan and confirm your arrangements in a written itinerary before arrival. Your provider determines the clinical requirements; we manage the non-clinical details around them.

03

Your recovery begins

Arrive

From the moment you land, your welcome, private transfer, local setup and accommodation are arranged so you can settle in with confidence.

04

Continuing support

Recover

Your Recovery Coordinator remains your point of contact throughout your stay — arranging transportation, meals, recovery essentials, housekeeping and other non-clinical needs as they arise, adapted to your care plan as it changes.

05

Optional experiences

Extend your stay Optional

With medical clearance, we arrange bespoke extensions on request — additional time to rest, a private villa or retreat, selected wellness experiences, or another destination suited to your plans and stage of recovery.

06

Continued care

Return home

After you return home, we assist with agreed follow-up arrangements, provider communication, virtual follow-up planning, records and other non-clinical details connected to your transition home.

The Experience

What we coordinate

Medical travel involves far more than the treatment itself. We bring the moving parts around your care together — preparation, arrival, appointments, recovery and your return home — with one goal: exceptional coordination and zero unnecessary friction.

Before departure

Your journey begins before takeoff

Your travel plans should work around your care, not the other way around. We bring together arrival timing, appointments, accommodation, recovery needs and return travel so your itinerary is in place before you leave.

On the ground

Private chauffeur transportation

Your journey continues beyond the airport. Private transportation is arranged around your itinerary — from arrival and accommodation to provider appointments, follow-up visits and your return to the airport.

Where you stay

A place designed for recovery

We arrange private, comfortable accommodation suited to your treatment, length of stay and preferred level of support — from well-appointed hotel suites to private residential or villa settings when appropriate.

During your stay

Support throughout your stay

Depending on your needs, services may include recovery-focused meals, nursing or personal assistance, pharmacy essentials, housekeeping, laundry and follow-up transportation — so you can focus on rest.

Behind the journey

Insurance & documentation support

Where applicable, we coordinate non-clinical documentation and communication around your journey, working alongside your insurer, assistance company or benefits administrator. Coverage, eligibility, reimbursement and pre-authorization remain subject to your policy and the insurer's approval.

After you return

Continuity once you are home

We coordinate departure, follow-up logistics, provider communication and records where appropriate — so the transition home is as considered as the arrival. Medical follow-up remains with your healthcare providers.

Why the Philippines

Reasons the Philippines stands apart

Internationally recognized healthcare

We partner with JCI-accredited healthcare institutions and facilities, reflecting established standards for patient safety and quality of care.

English-language fluency

English is the standard language across healthcare and professional settings, supporting clear communication through consultations, treatment and follow-up.

A culture of care & hospitality

Warmth, attentiveness and hospitality are deeply rooted in Filipino life — qualities that make the experience surrounding treatment feel more personal and supported.

Five destinations

Manila

Cebu

A major healthcare hub with the ease of island life.

Tagaytay

Highland calm, Taal views and cool mountain air.

Batangas

Coastal ease, heritage and countryside.

Palawan
