For Healthcare & Wellness Providers

Partner with Heal Before Home

We collaborate with carefully selected healthcare, wellness, recovery, hospitality and transportation providers to create a more seamless experience for international and discerning local clients seeking care in the Philippines.

Why partner with us

Support beyond the clinic walls.

A successful medical journey requires support beyond the clinic walls. By coordinating the non-clinical details that shape a patient's experience before, during and after treatment, we give your team greater freedom to remain focused on delivering excellent medical care, while your international patients receive a high-touch layer of logistical and recovery support.

Our role is to complement your work, never to replace or interfere with the provider-patient relationship. Think of us as your logistics, hospitality and recovery-support partner, coordinating accommodation, transportation, day-to-day arrangements, recovery support and the transition home around the care plan established by your team.

Partnership benefits

What a partnership looks like

Curated referrals

We introduce prospective clients whose needs and preferences may align with your expertise, scope of practice and standards.

Elevated patient experience

We coordinate the experience surrounding care, including accommodation, transportation, nutrition, recovery support and follow-up logistics where appropriate.

Premium brand alignment

Become part of a carefully considered network built around professionalism, patient safety, service quality and a high standard of client care.

Long-term partnerships

We prioritize trusted, long-term relationships with providers who value clear communication, quality care and a strong client experience.

The coordinated partnership framework

Where your role ends and ours begins.

Clinical care: your role

You remain responsible for assessment, diagnosis, treatment recommendations, medical decisions, clinical follow-up, and determining when your patient is ready to travel or resume activities.

HBH coordination: our role

We coordinate the non-clinical experience surrounding your care, including appointments, accommodation, private transportation, nutrition, recovery support, companion needs and other agreed services.

Continuity & transition

We coordinate schedules, follow-up logistics, provider communication, records where appropriate, and the client's transition home.

Heal Before Home does not provide medical treatment or clinical advice. All medical decisions remain solely between clients and their healthcare providers.

Who we partner with

Disciplines in our network

  • Healthcare providers: hospitals, medical centres, specialists, accredited clinics and diagnostic facilities
  • Dental care: general and restorative dentistry, cosmetic dentistry, oral surgery, dental implants, prosthodontics and specialty care
  • Hair restoration: hair transplantation, hair and scalp assessment, and regenerative hair-restoration providers
  • Reproductive care: fertility specialists, reproductive medicine, IVF providers and related diagnostic services
  • Interventional radiology: image-guided minimally invasive procedures, vascular interventions and advanced diagnostic imaging
  • Aesthetic, regenerative & reconstructive care: dermatology, aesthetic medicine, reconstructive care, selected surgical providers, and regenerative or technology-based treatments
  • Executive health & longevity: executive assessments, diagnostics, longevity programs, integrative care and selected wellness providers
  • Recovery & allied health: private nursing and caregiving, physiotherapy, nutrition and dietetics, lymphatic therapy and other allied health services
  • Hospitality & mobility: hotels, recovery-focused accommodations, private villas and retreats, private chauffeured transportation and other premium mobility providers
