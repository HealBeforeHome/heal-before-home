Areas of Care

Exceptional care deserves an exceptional journey.

These are the areas of care we coordinate around. Treatment is delivered by independent, qualified providers. We organize everything else.

Heal Before Home supports guests exploring care in the Philippines by introducing carefully considered provider options where needed, and by organizing the non-clinical arrangements around them: consultations and scheduling, accommodation, private transportation, recovery support, and follow-up logistics.

The appropriate treatment pathway in every area below is determined by your diagnosis, health status, history and a qualified specialist's assessment. Heal Before Home does not determine medical suitability or provide clinical advice.

What stays constant

The coordination around every area of care.

Whichever area brings you here, the surrounding journey is organized the same way, with one point of contact and a written itinerary confirmed before you travel.

  • Provider & appointment coordination: introductions where needed, consultations, diagnostics and scheduling
  • Travel & hospitality: accommodation, private transportation and itinerary planning around your provider's program
  • Recovery support: meals, nursing or personal assistance, housekeeping and day-to-day needs
  • Follow-up logistics: appointments, documentation, transportation and the transition home
