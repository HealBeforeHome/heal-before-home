Explore Your Cancer Care Options With a Second Opinion

A new diagnosis can bring urgent questions. You may wish to hear from another independent oncology specialist before deciding on your next steps. Heal Before Home can guide you through the practical arrangements for a virtual second opinion, wherever you are starting from.

Begin with a complimentary conversation about the second-opinion process. You may contact a provider directly or ask HBH to coordinate the arrangements. With your consent, our optional paid coordination service can assist with provider selection, records requests, scheduling, questions for the consultation and the steps that follow the specialist’s opinion.

The independent specialist reviews your case and makes all clinical recommendations. HBH does not assess whether immunotherapy is appropriate or advise you to delay time-sensitive treatment.

If treatment follows in the Philippines

Should your oncology team recommend treatment in the Philippines and clear you to travel, HBH can coordinate the arrangements around your appointments. A restorative stay, including a possible visit to Coron, may be considered separately when your treating specialists approve the timing and travel plan.
