Women’s Health

Women’s Health & Healthy Aging

Your health. Your confidence. Your choices.

Your health needs evolve through every stage of life. From preventive wellness and reproductive health to perimenopause, menopause, longevity and advanced aesthetic treatments, personalized care empowers you to understand your changing needs and explore possibilities that reflect your individual goals.

Heal Before Home coordinates the journey surrounding your independently selected care, with discretion and thoughtful attention to your individual needs.

Explore

Explore Women’s Health

Explore a range of medical, preventive, integrative and restorative options for women at different stages of life.

01

Perimenopause & Menopause

Individualized assessment and treatment options for the hormonal transitions of perimenopause and menopause, including symptoms affecting sleep, mood, energy and everyday well-being.

Care may include menopausal hormone therapy (HRT/MHT), regulated bioidentical hormone options, non-hormonal approaches and personalized symptom management.

02

Functional Medicine & Personalized Health

Naturopathic, functional and integrative approaches that consider nutrition, lifestyle, environmental influences and individual health history alongside appropriate medical assessment.

Care may address persistent fatigue, stress, digestive and immune concerns, hormonal health and personalized preventive strategies.

03

Hormonal & Metabolic Health

Assessment and management of hormonal and metabolic concerns, including thyroid health, insulin resistance, weight-related changes and conditions affecting energy and overall well-being.

Care may include laboratory investigations, specialist consultations and individualized treatment strategies.

04

Preventive Health & Women’s Screening

Personalized preventive assessments and age-appropriate screening, including breast and cervical health, cardiovascular risk, bone density and other areas of women’s preventive healthcare.

Services may include diagnostic testing, imaging, specialist consultations and personalized preventive health planning.

05

Bone, Muscle & Pelvic Health

Specialist assessment and treatment supporting bone density, muscle strength, joint health, pelvic floor function and physical well-being through different stages of life.

Care may include osteoporosis screening, pelvic floor physiotherapy, rehabilitation, strength and mobility programs, and individualized treatment for musculoskeletal concerns.

06

Gynecologic & Reproductive Health

Specialist assessment and treatment for menstrual, uterine, ovarian and reproductive health concerns, including gynecologic conditions that may require further investigation or surgical care.

Services may include diagnostic imaging, gynecologic consultations, minimally invasive procedures and individualized reproductive health planning.

07

Aesthetic & Reconstructive Care*

Surgical and non-surgical options addressing facial rejuvenation, skin health, breast and body concerns, and reconstruction.

Treatments may include regenerative aesthetics, collagen-stimulating procedures, facial and body contouring, and reconstructive interventions according to individual needs and clinical suitability.

08

Restorative Wellness & Healthy Aging

Personalized wellness experiences supporting rest, relaxation, movement, nourishment and overall well-being as individual needs evolve.

Services may include restorative wellness programs, traditional Filipino healing, therapeutic bodywork, nutrition and lifestyle support, and other wellness experiences according to individual preferences and medical clearance where appropriate.

Personalized Support

Confidence, Readiness & Life Transitions

Changes in your health, appearance and personal priorities can bring profound questions about what you want for the next chapter of your life.

Heal Before Home coordinates access to personalized coaching and professional psychological support tailored to your individual needs.

Our Well-Being First Protocol

For guests considering major elective cosmetic surgery, our coordination pathway requires an independent psychological readiness assessment and confidence and transition coaching before proceeding with surgical arrangements.

For non-surgical aesthetic treatments and medically necessary reconstructive procedures, coaching and psychological support may be coordinated according to your individual needs and healthcare provider’s recommendations.

Not every journey begins with yes. Your well-being comes first.
