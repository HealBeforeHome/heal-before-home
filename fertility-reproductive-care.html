Fertility & Reproductive Care in the Philippines

Fertility and reproductive care are highly personal, and the appropriate approach depends on your individual health, reproductive history, goals, and the clinical assessment of qualified healthcare professionals.

Care may involve diagnostic evaluation, fertility treatment, reproductive procedures, or other specialist services depending on your needs.

Heal Before Home coordinates the non-clinical arrangements surrounding fertility and reproductive care in the Philippines for international and discerning local guests.

From provider consultations and appointment scheduling to accommodation, private transportation, companion arrangements, and follow-up logistics, we organize the surrounding details around the plan established by your healthcare team.

Fertility & Reproductive Care Options

01

Fertility Assessment & Diagnostics

Comprehensive evaluations may help identify factors affecting fertility and guide conversations about possible treatment options.

02

In Vitro Fertilization (IVF)

IVF involves several stages of care that may include ovarian stimulation, egg retrieval, fertilization, embryo development, and embryo transfer under the direction of a fertility specialist.

03

Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)

IUI is a fertility treatment in which prepared sperm is placed directly into the uterus around the time of ovulation.

04

Egg & Embryo Freezing

Fertility-preservation options may include freezing eggs or embryos for potential future use following consultation and appropriate medical assessment.

05

Male Fertility Care

Evaluation may include testing and consultation to identify factors affecting sperm production, quality, or reproductive health and to discuss appropriate treatment options.

06

Reproductive Endocrinology

Evaluation and treatment of hormonal and reproductive conditions that may affect fertility, ovulation, menstrual cycles, ovarian function, or reproductive health, under the care of a qualified specialist.

07

Reproductive Surgery

Surgical treatment may be considered for certain conditions affecting fertility or reproductive health, such as fibroids, endometriosis, ovarian cysts, uterine abnormalities, or blocked fallopian tubes, following specialist assessment.

08

Other Specialized Services

Selected fertility centers in the Philippines may also offer ICSI, frozen embryo transfer (FET), preimplantation genetic testing (PGT-A), endometrial receptivity testing, fertility preservation and cryopreservation, oncofertility services, and specialized male and female fertility care. Availability depends on the provider, individual clinical needs, and applicable medical and regulatory requirements.

Before choosing a provider

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Provider Expertise & Treatment Approach

  • Provider Experience: What experience does the provider have with the type of fertility or reproductive care you are considering?
  • Treatment Approach: How is the recommended approach determined based on your health history, reproductive goals, and clinical findings?
  • Alternative Options: What other treatment or management options may be appropriate to consider?
  • Specialist Team: Will other specialists or laboratory professionals be involved in your care?

Testing, Timing & Treatment Planning

  • Diagnostic Testing: What testing or imaging may be required before treatment begins?
  • Treatment Timing: How does the treatment plan affect the timing and length of your stay in the Philippines?
  • Monitoring: How frequently will appointments, laboratory testing, imaging, or other monitoring be required?
  • Multiple Visits: Could the treatment require more than one trip or an extended stay?

Risks, Expectations & Follow-Up

  • Expected Outcomes: What outcomes are realistic for your individual circumstances?
  • Risks & Limitations: What potential risks, side effects, or limitations should you understand before proceeding?
  • Follow-Up: What follow-up will be required during your stay and after returning home?
  • Continuity of Care: How will records and treatment information be shared with your healthcare providers at home?

Understanding Your Options Matters

Your treating provider should discuss your individual circumstances, available options, expected outcomes, and potential risks so you can make an informed decision about your care.

Fertility treatment can involve important medical, emotional, financial, and logistical considerations. A thorough consultation should help you understand your diagnosis, available options, expected treatment timeline, realistic possibilities, potential limitations, and the follow-up care that may be required.
