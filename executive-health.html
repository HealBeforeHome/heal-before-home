Area of Care

Executive Health in the Philippines

Executive health programs provide a comprehensive view of your current health, risk factors, and areas that may warrant closer clinical attention.

The appropriate screening plan depends on your age, medical history, lifestyle, family history, existing conditions, and clinical assessment by qualified healthcare professionals.

Heal Before Home coordinates the non-clinical arrangements surrounding executive health and diagnostic programs in the Philippines for international and discerning local guests.

From appointment scheduling and accommodation to private transportation and follow-up logistics, we organize the details around your program so you can focus on your testing, consultations, and the recommendations provided by your healthcare team.

Executive Health & Diagnostic Options

01

Comprehensive Executive Physical

A comprehensive executive health assessment may combine physician consultation, laboratory testing, physical examination, health-risk assessment, and age- and risk-appropriate screening within a coordinated program.

02

Advanced Diagnostic Imaging

Depending on individual risk factors and provider recommendations, diagnostic assessment may include ultrasound, X-ray, CT, MRI, mammography, bone-density testing, or other imaging used to investigate specific health concerns or establish a broader health profile.

03

Cardiovascular Screening

Cardiovascular assessment may include blood-pressure evaluation, cholesterol and metabolic testing, electrocardiography, echocardiography, stress testing, vascular assessment, or other investigations based on individual cardiovascular risk.

04

Cancer Screening

Age-, sex-, family-history-, and risk-appropriate cancer screening may include selected laboratory tests, imaging, mammography, cervical screening, colorectal screening, prostate assessment, or other investigations recommended by the treating provider.

05

Metabolic & Endocrine Assessment

Evaluation may include blood glucose and diabetes screening, cholesterol and lipid testing, thyroid assessment, liver and kidney function, body-composition analysis, and other investigations related to metabolic and hormonal health.

06

Brain, Cognitive & Neurological Health

Where clinically appropriate, assessment may include neurological consultation, cognitive screening, memory evaluation, sleep-related assessment, imaging, or other investigations intended to identify factors affecting neurological and cognitive health.

07

Other Executive Health & Diagnostic Services

Additional services may include gastrointestinal screening, pulmonary-function testing, sleep assessments, bone-health evaluation, hearing and vision screening, nutritional assessment, genetic or hereditary-risk testing where offered, women’s and men’s health assessments, infectious-disease testing, specialist consultations, and other targeted diagnostics based on individual history, risk factors, and provider recommendations.

Before choosing an executive health provider

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Executive health programs can vary considerably in scope, diagnostic testing, clinical rationale, and physician oversight. A comprehensive program should be tailored to your individual risk factors rather than simply offering the broadest possible panel of tests. A thorough consultation should help you understand which screenings are appropriate for you, why they are being recommended, how your results will be reviewed, and what follow-up may be needed.

Clinical Oversight & Personalization

  • Medical Leadership: Who will oversee my executive health assessment, and what are their qualifications, board certifications, and relevant experience?
  • Screening Personalization: How will my age, medical history, family history, lifestyle, existing conditions, and individual risk factors influence the screening plan?
  • Routine vs. Targeted Testing: Which tests are being recommended specifically for me, and why? Are some tests routinely included even when they may not be appropriate for every individual?

Results Review & Additional Care

  • Physician Access: Will I have an opportunity to review my laboratory, imaging, and other diagnostic results directly with a physician?
  • Specialist Access: If a finding requires further investigation, can specialist consultations or additional diagnostic testing be arranged during my stay?
  • Results Timeline: When should I expect my laboratory findings, imaging reports, diagnostic results, and physician recommendations to be available?

Records & Follow Up

  • Records & Documentation: Will I receive copies of my laboratory results, imaging, reports, and physician recommendations for my personal records and healthcare providers at home?
  • Follow-Up Planning: What follow-up may be recommended after my executive health assessment, and which findings may require additional investigation or monitoring?
  • Continuity at Home: What information should be shared with my healthcare providers at home to support appropriate ongoing follow-up?
Planning your executive health assessment

Executive Health Assessment Phases & Logistics

Executive health screening may involve laboratory testing, diagnostic imaging, physician consultations, specialist appointments, and follow-up recommendations. Heal Before Home coordinates the surrounding non-clinical arrangements so the different components of your executive health assessment can unfold as one organized experience.

01

Phase 1

Before Your Assessment

The surrounding logistics are coordinated in advance around the screening plan and preparation instructions established by your healthcare team.

  • Appointment & Travel Coordination: Arranging consultations, diagnostic appointments, accommodation, and private transportation around your schedule.
  • Preparation & Scheduling: Organizing your itinerary around fasting requirements, testing sequences, or other preparation instructions provided by your healthcare team.
02

Phase 2

During Your Assessment

Day-to-day logistics are coordinated across clinics, laboratories, imaging facilities, and other healthcare locations involved in your program.

  • Multi-Provider Coordination: Organizing scheduling and transportation across physician consultations, diagnostic imaging, laboratory testing, and other appointments.
  • On-the-Ground Logistics: Keeping transportation, accommodation, appointments, and other arrangements organized so you do not have to manage multiple locations and schedules independently.
03

Phase 3

When Additional Testing is Recommended

Screening results may sometimes lead your physician to recommend additional diagnostic testing or specialist consultation while you are still in the Philippines.

  • Additional Testing & Consultations: Coordinating appointments for further imaging, laboratory testing, or specialist consultations recommended by your healthcare provider.
  • Schedule Adjustments: Updating accommodation, private transportation, and itinerary arrangements if additional appointments require more time.
04

Phase 4

Returning Home & Follow-Up

Before departure, Heal Before Home helps organize the non-clinical arrangements surrounding the follow-up plan provided by your healthcare team.

  • Results Review: Coordinating final consultations where your providers can review findings, explain recommendations, and discuss next steps with you.
  • Records & Documentation: Helping organize access to laboratory results, imaging files, reports, and physician recommendations for your records.
  • Follow-Up Coordination: Organizing remote consultations, future appointments, or additional testing recommended by your providers after you return home.
  • Continuity at Home: Helping organize the information and documentation you may need to share with your healthcare professionals at home.
Results & follow-up

Understanding What Comes Next Matters

An executive health assessment is most useful when the findings lead to clear next steps. Some guests may simply receive reassurance and preventive recommendations, while others may require further testing, specialist consultation, monitoring, or treatment.

Heal Before Home brings the next steps together based on your healthcare provider’s recommendations so important findings can be addressed during your time in the Philippines and after you return home.

Review Your Results

  • Your healthcare provider is responsible for interpreting your findings and explaining their clinical significance. Heal Before Home can help coordinate the appointments and logistics needed for that review.

We Arrange the Next Steps

  • If your provider recommends further imaging, laboratory testing, specialist consultation, rehabilitation, or another procedure during your stay, Heal Before Home can help coordinate those appointments, transportation, and scheduling.

Prepare for Follow-Up at Home

  • Before departure, Heal Before Home coordinates with your providers so you understand any recommended monitoring, further investigation, specialist care, or follow-up that should continue after you return home. We also help ensure you have access to relevant reports and information for your healthcare team at home.

Heal Before Home does not assess medical suitability, recommend or direct treatment, or replace your licensed healthcare providers. Where requested, we may help you explore vetted provider options and facilitate introductions for direct consultation and treatment planning.
