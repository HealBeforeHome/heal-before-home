Hair Restoration

Hair restoration is highly individual.

The appropriate approach depends on the cause and pattern of hair loss, donor-hair availability, scalp and hair characteristics, personal goals, and a comprehensive clinical assessment by a qualified provider.

Heal Before Home coordinates the non-clinical arrangements surrounding hair restoration treatment in the Philippines for international and discerning local guests.

From provider introductions and appointment scheduling to accommodation, private transportation, recovery support, and follow-up logistics, we organize the surrounding details so you can focus on your treatment and recovery.

Hair Restoration Options

01

FUE Hair Transplant

Individual hair follicles are extracted from a donor area and transplanted into areas of thinning or hair loss. FUE does not require removal of a strip of scalp and typically leaves small, less-visible extraction marks.

02

DHI Hair Transplant

A hair transplantation technique in which individual follicles are implanted using a specialized implanter tool, allowing precise control over the depth, angle, and direction of placement.

03

Non-Surgical Hair Restoration

May include PRP or PRF therapy, low-level laser therapy (LLLT), and prescribed topical or oral treatments designed to address thinning without transplant surgery.

04

Scalp Micropigmentation

A cosmetic treatment that deposits tiny points of pigment into the scalp to replicate the appearance of hair follicles, helping create the look of greater density or a closely shaved hairline.

05

Alopecia Treatments

Options depend on the type and cause of hair loss and may include topical or oral medications, corticosteroids, PRP/PRF, or other therapies following clinical evaluation.

06

Eyebrow & Beard Transplants

Hair follicles are transplanted from a donor area to the eyebrows or beard to restore sparse areas, improve definition, or create greater facial-hair density.

07

Other Hair & Scalp Treatments

Additional options may include exosome therapy, mesotherapy, medical hair-loss therapies, and comprehensive scalp and hair assessment.

Before choosing a hair restoration provider

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Hair restoration techniques can differ considerably in approach, donor-hair requirements, recovery, and expected results. A thorough consultation should help you understand which technique is being recommended, why it may be appropriate for you, the limitations of your donor supply, realistic outcomes, and what aftercare will be required.

Provider Expertise & Treatment Approach

  • Provider Credentials: Who will perform my procedure, and what are their qualifications, certifications, and experience with hair restoration?
  • Technique Selection: Which technique, such as FUE or FUT, is being recommended for me, and why?
  • Donor-Site Evaluation: How will my donor area, hair characteristics, and pattern of hair loss be evaluated before treatment?

Graft Planning & Expected Results

  • Graft Estimate: Approximately how many grafts are being recommended, and how was that number determined?
  • Donor-Hair Limitations: How much usable donor hair is available, and how might this affect future treatment options?
  • Realistic Expectations: What level of density and coverage may be realistic based on my current hair loss and donor supply?

Aftercare & Follow-Up

  • Early Aftercare: What instructions will I need to follow for washing, sleeping, physical activity, sun exposure, and protecting the treated area?
  • Follow-Up Schedule: Which checks or follow-up appointments will be required before I leave the Philippines?
  • Support After Returning Home: What follow-up is available once I return home, and who should I contact if I have questions or concerns?

Understanding Donor Supply Matters

A reputable hair restoration provider should be able to explain both the potential benefits and the limitations of treatment, including how your available donor supply may influence density, coverage, future procedures, and long-term planning.

Planning your journey

Hair Restoration Planning & Recovery Phases

Traveling for hair restoration involves more than the procedure itself. Heal Before Home coordinates the surrounding non-clinical arrangements around the treatment plan established by your provider, bringing appointments, transportation, accommodation, early recovery, and follow-up into one organized experience.

01

Phase 1

Before Your Treatment

The surrounding logistics are coordinated in advance around your treatment schedule and preparation instructions.

  • Provider & Appointment Coordination: Organizing consultations, procedure appointments, accommodation, and private transportation.
  • Pre-Treatment Planning: Structuring your itinerary around any preparation instructions or appointments required by your provider before treatment.
02

Phase 2

During Your Stay

Day-to-day logistics are coordinated around your procedure and scheduled follow-up.

  • Private Transportation: Organizing transportation to and from the clinic for your procedure and follow-up appointments.
  • On-the-Ground Coordination: Keeping accommodation, appointments, transportation, and companion arrangements organized throughout your stay.
03

Phase 3

Early Recovery & Aftercare

Recovery after hair restoration varies by individual and procedure. Your treating provider will give you specific instructions regarding graft protection, washing, sleeping position, activity, sun exposure, medication, and follow-up.

  • Recovery-Focused Accommodation: Arranging comfortable accommodation and day-to-day support around your provider’s aftercare instructions.
  • Follow-Up Coordination: Organizing transportation and scheduling for early scalp or graft checks requested by your treating provider.
  • Meal & Daily Support: Coordinating meals, transportation, companion support, and other non-clinical needs during the early recovery period.
04

Phase 4

Returning Home & Follow-Up

Before departure, Heal Before Home helps organize the non-clinical arrangements surrounding the follow-up plan established by your provider.

  • Final Provider Review: Coordinating appointments where your provider can review recovery, reinforce aftercare instructions, and discuss next steps.
  • Follow-Up Planning: Organizing remote consultations or future appointments recommended after you return home.
  • Records & Documentation: Helping organize access to treatment information, provider instructions, and other relevant documentation.
  • Return-Travel Logistics: Coordinating transportation and travel arrangements around your treatment schedule and your provider’s guidance regarding travel.
