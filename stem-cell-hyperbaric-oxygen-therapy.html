Stem Cell & Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy

Specialized Therapies. Thoughtfully Coordinated.

For individuals exploring regenerative medicine, stem cell therapies or hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT), Heal Before Home coordinates the practical journey surrounding independent specialist consultations and provider-supervised care in the Philippines.

From appointments and scheduling to accommodation, private transportation and recovery support, every arrangement is coordinated around the recommendations of your chosen healthcare professionals.

Stem Cell & Regenerative Medicine

Regenerative medicine is an evolving field that may include cell-based and biologic approaches depending on the individual, clinical indication, provider and applicable regulatory requirements.

HBH coordinates access to independent specialist consultations, giving clients an opportunity to understand the proposed therapy, its intended purpose, available evidence, potential risks, alternatives and anticipated follow-up before making an informed decision.

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy

Hyperbaric oxygen therapy involves breathing oxygen in a pressurized medical chamber under professional supervision.

HBOT is used for certain recognized medical conditions and may also be offered by some providers within broader recovery or wellness programs. Suitability, treatment protocols and the number of sessions required are determined by qualified healthcare professionals.

HBH coordinates the practical arrangements surrounding independently recommended HBOT, including scheduling, transportation, accommodation and recovery logistics.

Your Journey, Coordinated

Depending on the individual itinerary, HBH may coordinate independent specialist consultations and appointment scheduling; premium accommodation; private transportation; companion arrangements; provider-aligned nutrition and dietary coordination; practical recovery support; coordination between multiple appointments or destinations; and follow-up logistics before the return home.

Before you decide

Questions to Ask Your Provider

  • What exactly is the treatment being proposed, and what is its intended purpose?
  • Is this treatment established for my condition or considered investigational?
  • What evidence supports its use for my particular circumstances?
  • What are the potential risks, side effects and contraindications?
  • What qualifications and experience does the treating professional have?
  • If cells or biologic products are being used, what is their source and how are they processed?
  • What regulatory standards apply to the treatment, product and facility?
  • How many treatments or sessions may be required?
  • What should I expect during recovery?
  • What follow-up care will I require after returning home?
  • Are there medications, activities or travel plans I should discuss with my provider before or after treatment?

Before You Travel

Clinical suitability should be established directly with the treating provider before travel. Your provider may request your medical history, diagnostic results or additional assessments before confirming whether a particular therapy is appropriate.

For HBOT, certain medical conditions and circumstances can affect suitability. Appropriate clinical screening should therefore take place before treatment.

HBH’s Role

Heal Before Home provides non-clinical healthcare, recovery and dietary coordination for international clients.

HBH does not diagnose medical conditions, prescribe or recommend treatments, determine medical suitability or guarantee outcomes. Medical assessments, treatment recommendations, informed consent and clinical decisions remain between each client and their independently selected qualified healthcare professionals.

Some regenerative and stem cell applications may be investigational or supported by limited evidence for particular conditions. Availability and regulatory status may vary according to the therapy and provider.
