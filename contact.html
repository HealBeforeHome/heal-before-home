What happens next

Our role & your privacy

Heal Before Home is a Canada-based non-clinical coordination business. We arrange practical travel, hospitality and agreed recovery-support services surrounding independently provided healthcare.

HBH does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, clinical advice or determinations of medical suitability. These decisions remain with independent qualified healthcare professionals.

Non-clinical role
Privacy consent

Privacy & Discretion

Your Information. Handled With Care.

Privacy matters throughout your healthcare journey, from the first conversation to your return home.

Heal Before Home coordinates authorized information-sharing arrangements with your consent and according to the requirements of the independent healthcare providers involved. Where appropriate, we can arrange a medical-continuity package containing records supplied or authorized for release by your providers for your return-home care.

Please tell us if you would like to discuss an additional confidentiality agreement before sharing sensitive information. Clinical providers remain responsible for their own records and clinical decisions.
