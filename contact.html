What happens next

  • Initial enquiry. Tell us what you are considering. No medical records or sensitive health information are required at this stage.
  • Discovery conversation. We learn about your goals, provider status, travel plans, preferences, timing and the level of support you are looking for.
  • Journey planning. If needed, we introduce carefully considered provider options for you to explore directly, then build the coordination around the care plan they establish.

Heal Before Home does not determine medical suitability, provide medical treatment, or replace your licensed healthcare providers.
