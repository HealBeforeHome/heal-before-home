Corporate Offerings | Vancouver, Whistler & Okanagan

Signature Executive Decompression Frameworks

The Anti-Burnout Architecture for Elite Corporate Leadership

For leaders who need space to step back, HBH designs private, coordinated itineraries that may combine an independently delivered health assessment with movement, hospitality and restorative time. Each itinerary is tailored to the guest, the employer and the clinical provider’s recommendations.

These are proposed experiences. Supplier availability, professional services, clinical suitability and the exact inclusions are confirmed in a private proposal.

Clinical services and hospitality are quoted and invoiced separately. An eligible health expense may be reimbursable through a qualifying employer health spending arrangement; lifestyle spending depends on the employer’s plan and may be a taxable employee benefit. HBH does not determine tax or claims eligibility.

Package 1 | Vancouver, BC

The Urban Performance & Kinetic Golf Sanctuary

Private city stay  |  3 nights  |  Vancouver

A compact Vancouver experience for a leadership group seeking independent preventive assessment, time away from daily demands, and guided movement. Golf coaching and accommodation are arranged around the guest’s goals and availability.

  • Health assessment: A proposed Preventive Health Assessment with TELUS Health Care Centres. The provider confirms the tests, clinical recommendations and appointment location directly.
  • Stay: Three nights in confirmed suite categories at Rosewood Hotel Georgia, subject to availability.
  • Golf and movement: A proposed group session at University Golf Club, with a golf professional and an independently contracted kinesiologist or movement specialist where available. A separate indoor simulator session can be arranged as a weather alternative.
  • Meals and movement: Nutrition support based on preferences and any actual provider guidance; privately arranged ground transport and a professional driver, with the vehicle category confirmed in the proposal.
Package 2 | Whistler, BC

The Alpine High-Performance Reset

Active mountain seclusion  |  4 nights  |  Vancouver & Whistler

A mountain stay designed around movement, rest and unhurried conversation. An independent clinical baseline in Vancouver can be coordinated before travel, while the Whistler program is adapted to each guest’s fitness, season and preferences.

  • Clinical starting point: A proposed Vancouver preventive assessment, delivered and scoped by an independent provider.
  • Transfer: A proposed Harbour Air seaplane service or private seaplane charter between Vancouver and Whistler, where seasonal operations, weather, passenger needs and availability allow; private ground transfer is an alternative.
  • Stay: Four nights at Fairmont Chateau Whistler in an agreed Fairmont Gold room or suite category, subject to availability.
  • Movement: Private mobility sessions with a suitably credentialed practitioner, separately confirmed for the dates and scope.
  • Golf: A proposed Nicklaus North group experience, with lessons, club rentals and carts confirmed in advance. Golf is seasonal; the private proposal will specify a non-golf alternative.
  • Spa and meals: A proposed Scandinave Spa Whistler thermal visit or group booking, subject to capacity and suitability, with meal arrangements tailored to preferences and actual provider guidance.
Package 3 | Okanagan Valley, BC

The European Bio-Reset & Cryo Retreat

Lakeside restoration  |  4 nights  |  Vernon, Okanagan Valley

A quieter Okanagan itinerary with a proposed independent assessment in Vancouver followed by restorative time at Sparkling Hill Resort. Spa experiences can be selected around your preferences, schedule and individual suitability.

  • Assessment: An independently delivered preventive health assessment in Vancouver, with the chosen provider setting the test menu and timing.
  • Travel: Private ground transfer or a separately sourced regional flight with ground connections, subject to schedule and availability.
  • Stay: Four nights in a requested penthouse suite at Sparkling Hill Resort; its limited penthouse inventory requires confirmation before quoting.
  • KurSpa: A tailored sequence of available spa services and thermal facilities. Sparkling Hill offers a −110 °C Cryo Cold Chamber; use is optional and subject to the resort’s screening and individual suitability.
  • Rest and dining: Optional private mindfulness or sleep-coaching sessions with a separately contracted professional, and dining coordinated around dietary preferences and provider advice where given.
