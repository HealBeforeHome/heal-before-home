Corporate Allowances

Working Alongside Corporate Health & Wellness Benefits

HBH can work alongside an organization’s existing benefits structure, including Health Spending Accounts and Lifestyle Spending Accounts where applicable.

Health Spending Accounts

Certain qualifying healthcare expenses may be reimbursable through an employer-sponsored HSA, subject to applicable Canadian tax rules and plan terms.

Lifestyle Spending Accounts

Depending on plan design, employers may offer support for approved wellness, lifestyle and restorative experiences.

Eligibility and reimbursement are determined by the employer’s benefit plan and administrator. HBH does not determine tax eligibility or guarantee reimbursement.

An employer’s health, lifestyle or executive wellbeing benefits may contribute toward different parts of an experience. Each works differently.

Healthcare expenses — HSA or other health benefits

Depending on the plan, an HSA or another eligible health benefit may pay or reimburse qualifying healthcare expenses, such as approved diagnostic services or eligible medically necessary treatment. The clinical provider determines the service and supplies the relevant documentation. Cosmetic-only treatment generally does not qualify as an eligible medical expense.

The HBH experience — LSA or executive wellbeing allowance

Where an employer’s LSA or executive wellbeing program permits, it may fund or reimburse approved HBH coordination, hospitality, nutrition, fitness or restorative experiences. What a program covers, and whether the benefit is taxable, depends on the employer’s rules and the applicable tax rules.

A proposal that separates the details

HBH prepares a clear, itemized proposal: independent provider fees are shown separately from HBH coordination, accommodation, transportation and lifestyle elements, and companion costs are itemized separately from the executive’s own arrangements. Guests may submit the relevant items to their employer or benefits administrator for review.

Hotel, retreat, spa, leisure and companion expenses are not presumed to be reimbursable or tax-free. Eligibility, reimbursement and tax treatment are determined by each plan and the applicable rules; HBH does not decide claims eligibility or administer any HSA or LSA.
