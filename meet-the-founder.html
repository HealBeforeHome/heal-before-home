About the Principal

Meet the founder.

Agnes Dalisay is the Principal and foundational architect of Heal Before Home.

Having spent her life navigating both global environments and local operational spaces, Agnes developed an objective perspective, one grounded in rigorous international service standards while remaining deeply connected to Filipino culture, hospitality, and an innate instinct to care for others.

Recognizing that finding a medical specialist is only one component of a complex care journey, she established Heal Before Home to bridge the fragmentation separating clinical expertise from real-world travel, environmental recovery, and lifestyle logistics.

Agnes leads the strategic development of Heal Before Home, including provider relationships, operating standards, and the quality of its non-clinical partner network. Her focus is to bring the many moving parts of a healthcare journey into one coordinated ecosystem, reducing administrative friction so clients can focus on their health, recovery, and return home.
