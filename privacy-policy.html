Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Heal Before Home respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly and transparently.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, submit an inquiry, or otherwise interact with Heal Before Home.

Information we collect

We may collect personal information that you choose to provide to us, including your name, email address, telephone number, country of residence, travel or service interests, provider information, and any details you include in an inquiry or contact form.

Please do not submit highly sensitive medical information, medical records, financial information, passwords, or other confidential information through general website forms unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure process.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you use our website, such as your browser type, device information, IP address, pages visited, referring website, and general website activity. This information may be collected through cookies, analytics tools, or similar technologies.

How we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to your questions and inquiries.
  • Understand the type of healthcare, wellness, recovery, or travel support you are exploring.
  • Coordinate requested conversations or introductions.
  • Communicate with you about Heal Before Home services.
  • Improve our website, services, and user experience.
  • Maintain appropriate business and administrative records.
  • Comply with legal, regulatory, security, or fraud-prevention requirements.

Healthcare and wellness providers

Where you ask Heal Before Home to facilitate an introduction or coordination with a healthcare or wellness provider, relevant information may be shared with that provider when reasonably necessary to support your request.

Heal Before Home does not replace your healthcare provider. Medical consultations, diagnoses, treatment recommendations, informed consent, and treatment decisions remain between you and your chosen licensed healthcare provider.

Service providers

We may use trusted third-party service providers to support our website and business operations, including website hosting, customer relationship management, forms, communications, analytics, and administrative services. These providers may process limited personal information on our behalf and are expected to use that information only for the services they provide to us.

International processing

Heal Before Home operates across Canada and the Philippines and may work with service providers or partners in other jurisdictions. As a result, your personal information may be processed or stored outside the country where you live and may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to help the website function, understand how visitors use the site, and improve performance. You may be able to control or disable certain cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

Communications

If you choose to receive communications from Heal Before Home, we may contact you regarding your inquiry, requested services, relevant updates, or information about Heal Before Home. You may unsubscribe from promotional communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in the communication or by contacting us.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. However, no website, email system, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

How long we keep your information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to maintain appropriate business records, or to satisfy applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Your privacy choices

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information we hold about you, or to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may contact Heal Before Home to make a privacy-related request.

Third-party websites

Our website may contain links to healthcare providers, wellness providers, accommodations, transportation providers, or other third-party websites. Heal Before Home is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Children’s privacy

Heal Before Home’s website and services are not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through our website without appropriate authorization.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, business practices, or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how Heal Before Home handles personal information, please contact us through the Contact page on our website.
