Area of Care

Vision Care in the Philippines

Vision care encompasses medical and surgical services ranging from laser vision correction and cataract treatment to the management of glaucoma, retinal, corneal, and ocular surface conditions. The appropriate treatment depends on diagnosis, overall eye health, visual needs, personal goals, and specialist recommendation.

Heal Before Home connects discerning local and international guests with vetted vision-care providers in the Philippines while coordinating the non-clinical arrangements surrounding their care. This includes consultation scheduling, travel and accommodation planning, private transportation, recovery support, and follow-up logistics.

Vision Care Options

01

LASIK & Laser Vision Correction

Laser-based procedures designed to reshape the cornea and reduce dependence on glasses or contact lenses for eligible patients.

02

Cataract Surgery

Removal of a clouded natural lens and replacement with an artificial intraocular lens.

03

Premium Lens Replacement

Lens-based procedures using advanced intraocular lenses that may address cataracts, presbyopia, or selected refractive concerns.

04

Glaucoma Care

Diagnosis and treatment aimed at managing eye pressure and protecting the optic nerve.

05

Retinal Care

Diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the retina, including diabetic retinal disease, retinal tears, macular conditions, and other disorders.

06

Corneal Care

Diagnosis and treatment of conditions affecting the cornea, including infections, scarring, keratoconus, and other disorders.

07

Dry Eye & Ocular Surface Care

Diagnosis and treatment of chronic dry eye, irritation, inflammation, and other conditions affecting the eye surface.

08

Other Vision Services

Additional services may include comprehensive eye examinations, diagnostic imaging, pediatric ophthalmology, oculoplastic care, and other specialized ophthalmic services.

Before choosing a vision care provider

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Vision procedures differ in technique, technology, eligibility, recovery, and follow-up. Informed decisions require proper questions.

  • Specialist Experience: Ask about the specialist’s credentials, training, and experience performing your specific procedure, including how frequently they perform it.
