Area of Care

Vision & Ophthalmology

Precision care, effortlessly framed.

Ophthalmological and surgical eye services range from advanced laser correction and cataract treatments to the intricate management of retinal, corneal and glaucoma conditions. The optimal path rests entirely on precise diagnosis, your visual goals and your chosen specialist’s recommendation.

Heal Before Home choreographs the non-clinical framework surrounding your independent ophthalmic care in the Philippines — discreetly coordinating travel logistics, premium accommodation, private transportation and dedicated recovery support.

Informed choices begin with the right questions

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Your chosen ophthalmologist should guide all clinical decisions, including treatment suitability, alternatives and anticipated outcomes.

Questions to discuss with your specialist

  • What is my diagnosis, and which treatment options are appropriate for my condition?
  • What diagnostic tests are required before making a decision?
  • What are the potential benefits, risks and alternatives?
  • Am I a suitable candidate for the proposed procedure?
  • What visual outcomes can reasonably be expected?
  • Will I require additional treatment or corrective lenses afterward?
  • What is the anticipated recovery period?
  • When will I be medically cleared to fly?
  • What follow-up appointments will be necessary after returning home?

Your Journey, Thoughtfully Coordinated

Depending on your individual itinerary and provider recommendations, HBH may coordinate:

  • Independent specialist consultations and appointment scheduling.
  • Premium accommodation selected with comfort and accessibility in mind.
  • Private transportation for consultations, procedures and follow-up visits.
  • Companion and practical post-procedure support.
  • Provider-aligned nutrition and dietary coordination.
  • Follow-up logistics before your return home.

All arrangements are planned around your independently selected provider’s clinical guidance and recommended recovery timeline.

Before You Travel

Certain ophthalmic procedures may require multiple assessments, post-treatment reviews or temporary travel restrictions.

Your treating ophthalmologist must determine your clinical suitability, required length of stay and fitness to fly. HBH coordinates the surrounding travel arrangements once these requirements have been established.

HBH’s Role

Heal Before Home provides non-clinical healthcare, recovery and dietary coordination for international clients.

HBH does not diagnose eye conditions, recommend procedures, determine treatment suitability or guarantee visual outcomes. All medical assessments, treatment recommendations, informed consent and clinical decisions remain between each client and their independently selected qualified healthcare professionals.
