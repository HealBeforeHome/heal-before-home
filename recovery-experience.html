The Recovery Experience

Everything Around Your Care, Coordinated

Your treatment plan belongs to your healthcare provider. The environment around it — where you stay, how you move, who supports you, what happens between appointments and how you prepare to return home — should feel just as considered. Heal Before Home brings those moving parts together around your provider-led care.

Recovery begins with the environment around you

Recovery is not one service or one room. It is the combination of practical details that allow you to rest, move through appointments and follow your provider’s instructions with fewer distractions. We coordinate those details before you arrive and adapt them as your needs change.

Arrival & Stay

From arrival to settling in

Your recovery environment begins with a well-planned arrival. Where included in your journey, airport-authorized VIP assistance is arranged in advance, followed by a personal welcome from your Heal Before Home coordinator and private chauffeur transfer. From there, your accommodation is considered around your treatment schedule, privacy preferences, mobility needs, companion or caregiver requirements, and proximity to follow-up care.

01

VIP Airport Assistance

Airport-authorized VIP assistance is arranged in advance, providing dedicated support through the arrival process and a more streamlined transition through the terminal before you are personally welcomed by your Heal Before Home coordinator.

02

HBH Meet & Greet

Your coordinator welcomes you at the designated meeting point and oversees your transition to private transportation and accommodation.

03

Arrival Essentials

Before leaving the airport, your coordinator assists with practical needs such as SIM or eSIM activation, local connectivity and access to appropriate currency exchange or ATM services.

04

Private Chauffeur Transfer

Once arrival formalities and essentials are complete, your private chauffeur takes you directly to your accommodation.

05

Recovery-Focused Accommodation

Your stay is selected around comfort, privacy, accessibility and the practical requirements of your recovery.

06

HBH Welcome Amenity

A thoughtful welcome awaits at your accommodation, with selected comforts and locally inspired touches prepared to help you settle in from the moment you arrive.

For higher-touch journeys, a curated basket of locally inspired comforts and thoughtful essentials may be prepared for your arrival.

Accommodation

Private Villa-Style Stays

The privacy of a personal retreat, with the amenities of a premium hotel.

Your stay is selected to complement your private health and wellness itinerary: a serviced suite at Ascott BGC, an expansive private residence at Shangri-La The Fort, or a luxury suite at Nüwa Hotel at City of Dreams. HBH coordinates the arrangements around your preferences, companions and provider’s recommendations.

For guests who value discretion, we can request a property-approved parking-to-room arrival route and coordinate movement with the hotel team, subject to its security and access policies. Your personal suite is also prepared for agreed nutrition, ergonomic support and other in-room details before arrival.

A Signature HBH Detail

The HBH Wedge Pillow System

Prepared for your arrival. Yours to take home.

Before you arrive, we unbox a brand-new HBH Wedge Pillow System exclusively for you and prepare it in your suite, ready for use throughout your stay. Its inclined positioning may be arranged for post-operative elevation and respiratory comfort, where appropriate and in accordance with your healthcare provider’s recommendations.

Your pillow is never reused or shared. When it is time to return home, we pack that same pillow in your signature HBH duffel bag — a complimentary gift that extends a thoughtful part of your HBH experience beyond your stay.

Behind the scenes

The Details You Should Not Have to Chase

From appointment confirmations and itinerary adjustments to transportation changes and practical follow-through, Heal Before Home manages the details that keep your experience moving seamlessly. One point of coordination means you can focus on your health and recovery while we attend to everything surrounding it.

When Plans Change

Travel and healthcare plans do not always unfold as expected. Should appointments shift, additional consultations be recommended, or recovery require more time, Heal Before Home adjusts the surrounding arrangements — keeping your accommodation, transportation, and support aligned with your changing needs.

Built Around Your Journey

No two journeys are alike. Whether you need support with a single aspect of your stay or prefer a fully coordinated experience, Heal Before Home tailors each arrangement to your needs, preferences, and healthcare provider’s recommendations.
