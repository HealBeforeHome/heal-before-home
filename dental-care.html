Area of Care

Dental Care in the Philippines

Dental care can range from preventive and restorative services to implants, oral surgery, and complex full-mouth rehabilitation. The appropriate approach depends on your oral health, existing dental work, bone and gum condition, treatment goals, and clinical assessment.

Heal Before Home guides international guests in exploring dental providers in the Philippines while arranging the non-clinical details around treatment: from consultations and planning to transportation, accommodation, recovery, follow-up, and return home.

Dental Care Options

01

Dental Implants

Dental implants replace one or more missing teeth using implant fixtures placed in the jawbone and restored with crowns, bridges, or implant-supported prosthetics following appropriate assessment and treatment planning.

02

Full-Arch Implant Rehabilitation

Full-arch treatment may replace most or all teeth in an upper or lower arch using multiple dental implants to support a fixed prosthesis. Approaches may include All-on-X protocols, with the number and placement of implants determined by the treating provider.

03

Crowns, Bridges & Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

Crowns and bridges can restore damaged or missing teeth, while more comprehensive rehabilitation may combine restorative, prosthodontic, implant, and aesthetic treatment to rebuild function and appearance.

04

Veneers & Smile Design

Porcelain or composite veneers may improve tooth shape, proportion, color, and alignment as part of an individualized smile design developed with a qualified dental provider.

05

Root Canal & Restorative Care

Root canal treatment may preserve teeth affected by infection or significant damage. Restorative care may also include fillings, inlays, onlays, post-and-core treatment, and other procedures intended to restore damaged teeth.

06

Periodontal Care

Treatment for gum disease and the supporting structures of the teeth may include deep cleaning, scaling and root planing, periodontal therapy, crown lengthening, and other procedures intended to protect gum health and preserve teeth.

07

Oral Surgery & Extractions

Oral surgical care may include simple or surgical extractions, wisdom-tooth removal, bone grafting, selected implant-related procedures, biopsies, and other surgery based on clinical need and specialist assessment.

08

Other Dental Services

Additional services available through selected Philippine providers may include orthodontics and clear aligners, dentures and implant-supported dentures, teeth whitening, preventive dentistry, professional cleaning, pediatric dentistry, TMJ assessment and splints, night guards, apicoectomy, cosmetic bonding, and other general or specialized dental treatments.

Before choosing a dental care provider

Know What to Ask Before You Decide

Dental treatment plans can vary significantly depending on your diagnosis, oral health, the procedure, materials used, and the amount of restorative or preparatory work required. A thorough consultation should help you understand the recommended plan, available alternatives, expected timeline, potential risks, recovery, and long-term maintenance.

Diagnosis & Treatment Planning

  • Personalized Recommendation: Ask why the proposed treatment is appropriate for your oral health, anatomy, treatment needs, and long-term goals.
  • Alternative Options: Understand the available alternatives, their potential benefits and limitations, and what may happen if treatment is postponed or not pursued.
  • Realistic Expectations: Discuss the results that may reasonably be expected based on your teeth, gums, jawbone, overall oral health, and the proposed treatment.

Materials & Preparatory Procedures

  • Materials & Systems: For implants or major restorations, ask which implant systems, materials, crowns, or prosthetic options will be used and why they are being recommended.
  • Preparatory Work: Clarify whether extractions, bone grafting, sinus lifts, periodontal treatment, or other preparatory procedures may be required before the primary treatment.

Timelines & Treatment Stages

  • Treatment Stages: Understand whether your treatment will be completed in one visit or in phases, such as surgical placement, healing, and final restoration.
  • Time Commitment: Ask how many appointments are anticipated, how much healing time may be required between stages, and whether a return visit to the Philippines could be necessary.

Risks, Aftercare & Follow-Up

  • Potential Risks: Discuss possible complications and limitations associated with your specific treatment.
  • Immediate Aftercare: Ask what recovery instructions, medications, activity restrictions, and follow-up appointments your treating provider recommends while you are in the Philippines.
  • Long-Term Follow-Up: If treatment is staged or requires ongoing monitoring, clarify what follow-up will be needed after you return home and what information should be shared with your dentist at home.

Recovery & aftercare

Prioritizing Your Recovery in the Philippines

Understanding what comes next matters. Recovery timelines vary depending on your procedure.

Some treatments require little downtime, while dental implants, bone grafting, extractions, periodontal surgery, or full-mouth rehabilitation may involve longer healing periods and additional follow-up before you travel home.

Your dental provider directs your clinical recovery and determines when you are ready to travel. Heal Before Home coordinates the non-clinical arrangements around that plan, helping keep your stay organized and supported while you recover.

Following Your Provider’s Recovery Plan

  • Medication & Pain Relief: Prescribed medications, antibiotics, and pain-management instructions.
  • Swelling & Oral Hygiene: Guidance for managing swelling and caring for the treatment area during early healing.
  • Diet & Activity: Temporary food, exercise, or activity restrictions appropriate to your procedure.
  • Temporary Restorations: Instructions for protecting temporary crowns, bridges, dentures, or other restorations while healing.

Coordinating On-the-Ground Support

  • Recovery-Focused Accommodation: Comfortable lodging selected with rest, convenience, and proximity to follow-up care in mind.
  • Tailored Meal Arrangements: Soft-food, liquid, or other meal options coordinated around dietary guidance from your treating team.
  • Pharmacy & Transportation Support: Prescription pickups and private transportation to follow-up appointments when needed.
  • Companion & Day-to-Day Support: Practical assistance for you or a companion during the recovery period.

Preparing to Return Home

  • Travel Readiness After Dental Treatment: Your treating provider determines when you are medically ready to travel and communicates any relevant precautions.
  • Aftercare Instructions: Helping ensure the medication, oral-care, dietary, and activity instructions provided by your dental team are available before departure.
  • Follow-Up Planning: Clarifying the appointments, monitoring, or future treatment stages recommended after you return home.
  • Records & Documentation: Coordinating access to relevant imaging, treatment summaries, and other records for your dentist or specialist at home.
Meal planning during recovery

Soft Food Ideas During Dental Recovery

Food choices and progression should follow the instructions provided by your treating dental team.

Early Recovery

  • Protein: Greek yogurt, smooth protein drinks, silken tofu, lukewarm broth.
  • Carbohydrates & Grains: Smooth soups, cream of wheat, blended oatmeal.
  • Fruits & Vegetables: Applesauce, seed-free smoothies eaten with a spoon, blended vegetable purées.

As Your Provider Allows More Texture

  • Scrambled eggs, soft flaky fish, tender shredded chicken, soft beans.
  • Mashed potatoes, well-cooked pasta, oatmeal, soft pancakes.
  • Mashed avocado, ripe banana, soft cooked carrots or squash.

Foods & Habits to Avoid

  • Straws or suction
  • Hard or crunchy foods
  • Sticky or seedy foods
  • Spicy or acidic foods
  • Very hot foods or beverages
  • Anything that may irritate or disturb the treatment area
