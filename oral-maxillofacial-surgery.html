Area of Care

Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Specialized surgical care for the mouth, jaw and face, addressing complex dental conditions, facial structure and function.

From corrective jaw surgery and complex tooth extractions to facial reconstruction and advanced implant procedures, Heal Before Home coordinates specialist consultations and the arrangements surrounding your treatment, recovery and return home in the Philippines.

Explore

Surgical Procedures & Treatments

01

Corrective Jaw Surgery*

Surgical correction of jaw alignment and structural irregularities affecting facial balance, bite and function.

Procedures may include upper or lower jaw repositioning, orthognathic surgery and correction of skeletal differences affecting chewing, speaking or breathing.

02

Complex Tooth Extractions

Surgical removal of impacted wisdom teeth, deeply positioned teeth and teeth requiring advanced extraction techniques.

Treatment may involve specialist assessment, diagnostic imaging and individualized surgical planning.

03

Advanced Implant Surgery & Bone Grafting

Surgical procedures supporting complex dental restoration, including bone grafting, sinus lifts, advanced implant placement and reconstruction of areas with insufficient jawbone.

Treatment may involve specialized surgical planning and preparation for fixed dental restorations.

04

Salivary Gland Surgery

Specialist assessment and surgical management of conditions affecting the salivary glands, including persistent swelling, blockages, stones and selected gland abnormalities.

Treatment may include diagnostic imaging, minimally invasive procedures or surgical intervention according to individual clinical findings.

05

Facial & Jaw Reconstruction*

Reconstructive procedures addressing structural differences, injuries and conditions affecting the mouth, jaw and facial bones.

Treatment may involve bone reconstruction, corrective surgery and restoration of facial form and function.

06

TMJ (Temporomandibular Joint) & Jaw Disorders

Specialist assessment and treatment of conditions affecting the temporomandibular joint (TMJ), which connects the lower jaw to the skull and enables jaw movement.

Care may address jaw pain, clicking, locking, restricted movement and joint dysfunction through diagnostic imaging, non-surgical management or selected surgical procedures when clinically indicated.

07

Oral Pathology & Jaw Cysts

Specialist assessment and management of cysts, lesions and other abnormalities affecting the mouth, jaw and surrounding tissues.

Procedures may include diagnostic biopsies, surgical removal and further treatment according to clinical findings.

08

Facial Trauma Surgery

Surgical assessment and management of injuries involving the facial bones, jaw, teeth and surrounding structures.

Treatment may include fracture repair, reconstruction and procedures addressing facial alignment and function.

Personalized Support

Our Well-Being First Protocol

Facial and jaw surgery can involve significant changes to your appearance, function and sense of self.

For guests considering major elective cosmetic procedures, including cosmetic corrective jaw surgery and elective facial reconstruction, Heal Before Home requires an independent psychological readiness assessment and confidence and transition coaching before proceeding with surgical arrangements.

For medically necessary or reconstructive procedures, psychological support and coaching may be coordinated according to your individual circumstances and healthcare provider’s recommendations.

Not every journey begins with yes. Your well-being comes first.

* The asterisks on Corrective Jaw Surgery and Facial & Jaw Reconstruction indicate that this protocol applies only when the procedure is major and primarily elective or cosmetic. They do not mean that every medically necessary jaw or reconstruction patient requires coaching.

How Heal Before Home supports your surgical stay

Care Beyond the Appointment

Heal Before Home manages the non-clinical arrangements surrounding maxillofacial surgery so you can focus on treatment and recovery rather than logistics.

Depending on your needs, services may include:

Scheduling & Coordination Support

  • Provider introductions
  • Specialist consultation and appointment coordination
  • Diagnostic imaging appointment coordination
  • Pre-arrival and itinerary planning
  • Scheduling across multiple treatment stages
  • Coordination of provider-required follow-up appointments
  • Practical planning for staged treatment or future return visits

Travel, Hospitality & Recovery Support

  • Airport and private transportation
  • Comfortable, recovery-focused accommodation
  • Transportation between surgical and follow-up appointments
  • Companion arrangements where appropriate
  • Meal coordination around post-surgical dietary requirements
  • Medication and recovery-supply coordination where appropriate
  • Preparation for your journey home
